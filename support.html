SayFit Support

SayFit logs meals and workouts by voice and puts them in Apple Health. Email: om@turgon.ai. We answer within two working days.

Quick fixes

The mic button does nothing. Settings > SayFit > turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. iOS asks once; if you said no, this is where to change it.

Entries don't show up in the Health app. Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > SayFit > turn on every "Write" toggle (Workouts, Active Energy, Dietary Energy, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Fat, Fiber). Entries you already logged are kept in SayFit and are re-written to Health the next time you log.

The coach never calls. Profile > Coach calls > pick at least one time. Calls only ring at the times you picked. Also check Settings > SayFit > Notifications is on, and that Focus mode allows SayFit (time-sensitive notifications).

The coach cuts me off or doesn't hear me. It waits for a real pause, so just keep talking. If it says "Sorry, say that once more?" twice in a row, you probably have a weak connection: the call needs internet.

"Turn the volume up" shows on the call screen. Press the volume-up button during the call. Call volume is separate from media volume on iPhone.

It logged the wrong thing. Tap the entry on Home, fix the number, or swipe to delete. You can also say the fix on a call: "actually that was two eggs, not three."

I want everything gone. Delete the app. Then Health > Profile > Apps > SayFit > Delete All Data from SayFit. See the privacy policy for what we keep (nothing about you).

What SayFit sends off your phone

Only your voice while you talk, so it can be understood. See the privacy policy: https://omdivyatej.github.io/voicefit/privacy

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, a microphone, an internet connection. Apple Health is optional but recommended.
