MealSaid Support

MealSaid logs meals and workouts by voice and puts them in Apple Health. Email: om@turgon.ai. We answer within two working days.

Quick fixes

The mic button does nothing. Settings > MealSaid > turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. iOS asks once; if you said no, this is where to change it.

Entries don't show up in the Health app. Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > MealSaid > turn on every "Write" toggle (Workouts, Active Energy, Dietary Energy, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Fat, Fiber). Entries you already logged are kept in MealSaid and are re-written to Health the next time you log.

The app never calls. Profile > Scheduled calls > pick at least one time. Calls only ring at the times you picked. Also check Settings > MealSaid > Notifications is on, and that Focus mode allows MealSaid (time-sensitive notifications).

The call cuts me off or doesn't hear me. It waits for a real pause, so just keep talking. If it says "Sorry, say that once more?" twice in a row, you probably have a weak connection: the call needs internet.

"Turn the volume up" shows on the call screen. Press the volume-up button during the call. Call volume is separate from media volume on iPhone.

It logged the wrong thing. Tap the entry on Home, fix the number, or swipe to delete. You can also say the fix on a call: "actually that was two eggs, not three."

I want everything gone. Profile > Delete account > confirm. That removes your account and everything about you from our servers and wipes the app on this phone. Then Health > Profile > Apps > MealSaid > Delete All Data from MealSaid. See the privacy policy for what we keep.

Your account

MealSaid asks you to sign in with Apple the first time you open it (one tap, no password). Your log is saved to that account so it is still there after a new phone or a reinstall. "Hide My Email" works fine.

Sign out (Profile tab) keeps your account on our servers; this phone forgets the log. Sign in again and it comes back.

Delete account (Profile tab) deletes the account and every row about you from our servers, signs you out, and wipes the phone. It cannot be undone. Entries already written to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there (see above).

New phone? Install MealSaid and sign in with the same Apple ID. Your log, goals and call times are downloaded.

Notifications

Besides the scheduled call, we may occasionally send a notification about a new feature or to ask for feedback. Turn these off in Settings > MealSaid > Notifications.

What MealSaid sends off your phone

Your voice while you talk, so it can be understood, and your log entries, goals and call times, so they are saved to your account. Apple Health data is never uploaded. See the privacy policy: https://omdivyatej.github.io/mealsaid-site/privacy.html

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, a microphone, an internet connection. Apple Health is optional but recommended.
