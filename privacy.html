MealSaid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. MealSaid (bundle id ai.turgon.voicefit) is made by Turgon. Contact: om@turgon.ai.

The short version

You talk, we turn it into a food or workout entry, and the entry lives on your phone and in Apple Health. We do not have accounts, we do not know who you are, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

What leaves your phone

When you hold the mic button or take a call from the app, your voice is sent to our server so it can be understood. Two companies help with that:

  • Deepgram turns the audio into text (speech recognition).
  • Anthropic turns the text into a log entry and writes the app's reply (language model).

Our server runs on Fly.io in Singapore. Deepgram and Anthropic receive only what they need (the audio or the text), only to do the job above, and under API terms that do not let them use it to train their models or for their own purposes.

During a call the app also sends a few numbers as context: today's calories and macros, your daily targets, and the time of day. That is so the app can say "you're 400 calories under" rather than guessing.

What never leaves your phone

  • Everything in Apple Health. We read workouts, steps, weight, heart rate and sleep on the device and write meals and workouts on the device. None of it is uploaded.
  • Your log history, your profile (weight, height, goals) and your call schedule. They are stored on your phone only.
  • Your name, email, phone number or any identifier. We do not ask for them and we do not generate one.

How long we keep things

Audio is processed in memory and discarded. We do not store recordings. Our server keeps ordinary request logs (timestamps, status codes, the text it received) for up to 30 days to fix bugs, then they are deleted. Deepgram and Anthropic keep API data for their own abuse monitoring for a limited period under their published policies (Deepgram: not retained by default for API customers; Anthropic: up to 30 days unless flagged for policy violations) and then delete it.

The synthesized voice lines are cached on our server as audio files. They contain no user data (they are the app's words, not yours).

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything on the phone is gone. To remove what the app wrote to Apple Health, open Health > Profile > Apps > MealSaid > Delete All Data from MealSaid. There is nothing to delete on our side because nothing is tied to you, but if you want us to purge server logs for a time window, email om@turgon.ai with the approximate times and we will.

Tracking and analytics

None. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter of our own. We do not use the Apple advertising identifier.

Children

MealSaid is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and note it in the App Store "What's New".
